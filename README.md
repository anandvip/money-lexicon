# money-lexicon

**Lexicon of Modern Money Habits**: invented words for everyday money behaviours, from *spendulum* to *mandi-mirage*. It's a single-file study page with word cards you can filter, the four ways the words were built, a blend workshop, a scenario quiz, and recall checks.

Live page: [anandvip.github.io/money-lexicon](https://anandvip.github.io/money-lexicon/)

- `index.html` is the whole page in one file. It loads Google Fonts and nothing else.
- All coined words are original inventions and are labelled as such. Real-world terms and idioms were checked against sources, and the result of each check is shown on the page.

Created 18 Nov 2026 (IST).
